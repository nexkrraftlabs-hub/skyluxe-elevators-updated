Product Overview

Pneumatic Elevator

The Skyluxe Pneumatic Elevator runs on air pressure technology in a sleek, space-saving cylindrical design. It fits into tight spaces and needs very little shaft and pit work, which makes it a neat solution for homes that were not built with a lift in mind.

Low power consumption and quick installation make it a practical way to bring easy movement between floors to duplex homes, bungalows and private residences.

  • Space-saving cylindrical design for tight spaces
  • Works on air pressure technology with low power consumption
  • Quick installation with minimal shaft and pit requirements
  • Perfect for duplex homes, bungalows, and private residences
