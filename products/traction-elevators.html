Product Overview

Traction Elevators

The Skyluxe Traction Elevator is built around the needs of healthcare buildings. Its extra-wide cabin makes it easy to move stretchers, wheelchairs and medical equipment, while smooth acceleration and deceleration keep every trip comfortable for patients.

Anti-skid flooring and strong handrails add everyday safety for patients, visitors and medical staff alike.

  • Extra-wide cabin to accommodate stretchers and wheelchairs
  • Smooth acceleration and deceleration for patient comfort
  • Anti-skid flooring and strong handrails for safety
  • Suitable for hospitals, clinics, and healthcare centers
