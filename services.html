Our Services

From first installation to lifelong maintenance, we keep your elevators running safely

Installation Services

Complete elevator installation by trained technicians, from shaft inspection to final handover and safety certification.

  • Site survey and shaft measurement before work begins
  • Certified technicians and tested components
  • Clean, on-schedule installation with minimal disruption
  • Load testing and safety checks before handover

AMC & Maintenance

Scheduled servicing that keeps your lift reliable, quiet and compliant, so small faults are fixed before they become breakdowns.

  • Regular inspection of motor, ropes, doors and controller
  • Lubrication, adjustment and wear-part checks
  • Service report after every visit
  • Flexible plans for homes, offices and housing societies

Emergency Repairs

Lift stuck or not working? Our response team is on call to get people out safely and get the elevator back in service.

  • Round-the-clock breakdown support line
  • Quick dispatch of the nearest technician
  • Trapped-passenger rescue handled safely
  • Root-cause check so the fault does not repeat

Modernization

Upgrade an ageing elevator instead of replacing it, with newer controls, doors and cabin finishes that improve safety and ride quality.

  • New controller and drive for a smoother ride
  • Modern door operators and safety sensors
  • Refreshed cabin interiors and lighting
  • Lower energy use and fewer breakdowns

Consultation Services

Not sure which elevator suits your building? Get practical advice on type, capacity, space and budget before you commit.

  • On-site assessment of shaft, pit and headroom
  • Capacity and speed planning for your traffic
  • Clear comparison of options and costs
  • Help with approvals and safety requirements

Spare Parts Supply

Genuine, tested spare parts for most elevator types, so repairs are not delayed waiting for a component.

  • Door parts, buttons, sensors, ropes and controllers
  • Parts matched to your lift model and capacity
  • Ready stock of fast-moving components
  • Supply-only or supply-and-fit options
